PRIVACY AT KINESIS

We only use information needed to operate the website and its member features.

This Privacy Policy explains how KINESIS Organization handles information when you browse the website, create an account, use member features, contact the organization, or interact with website analytics.

1. Overview

KINESIS Organization (“KINESIS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of visitors and members who use this website.

This policy applies to information handled through the KINESIS website. It does not replace the privacy policies of third-party services that may be used by the website.

2. Information We Handle

Account information

When you create an account, the website may use your name and email address to create and display your member profile.

Authentication credentials are processed through Firebase Authentication. KINESIS does not receive or store your plaintext password.

Profile information

If you choose to complete or update your profile, information such as your display name and bio may be stored for your member account.

Event or activity information

When event registration features are available, the website may store details such as your name, email address, account identifier, event identifier, and registration timestamp.

Contact information

If you contact KINESIS, we may receive the name, email address, inquiry category, subject, message, and any other information you voluntarily provide.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled through this website may be used to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage member accounts.
  • Display and update member profile information.
  • Provide access to member-only pages and resources.
  • Manage event or activity registration when such features are available.
  • Respond to inquiries, questions, and collaboration requests.
  • Maintain website functionality, security, and user experience.
  • Understand general website usage through analytics.

KINESIS does not sell personal information to advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

The website uses third-party services to provide some functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

  • Google Firebase: used for authentication and database-related website features.
  • Google Analytics: used to understand general website usage and performance.
  • Google Maps: may be embedded on the Contact page to display the KINESIS location.
  • Google Fonts: used to load website typography.
Opening external services such as Messenger, Facebook, Gmail, or Google Maps may take you to another website or app governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy practices.

5. Analytics & Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as visits, pages viewed, device or browser information, and other technical usage data made available through the analytics service.

Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the visitor’s browser, device, region, and the configuration of the service.

You can control or delete browser cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some technologies may affect certain website features.

6. Data Retention

Account, profile, registration, and inquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate the relevant website feature, maintain records, resolve issues, or meet legitimate organizational needs.

If you request account or data deletion, KINESIS will review the request and remove information that can reasonably be deleted from systems under its control, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

7. Security

KINESIS uses third-party infrastructure such as Firebase for account authentication and website data services. Reasonable efforts are made to protect information, but no website or online system can guarantee absolute security.

Members should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing passwords or authenticated account access with other people.

8. Your Choices

Depending on the website feature, you may be able to review or update profile information while signed in.

You may contact KINESIS to ask about your account information or to request correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account.

To make a privacy-related request, email kinesisorganization@gmail.com .

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the latest published version.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related requests, contact KINESIS Organization using the Contact page or the email address below.
